# M1 — Audio (sceAudio output, SAS synthesis, sceAtrac) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (inline) or superpowers:test-driven-development per task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Patapon 3 plays sound: SAS-synthesized effects/voices and ATRAC3plus music reach the PC's speakers through `sceAudio`, with the PSP's blocking-output pacing driven by the real audio device.

**Architecture:** three independent pieces with pure, unit-tested cores and thin HLE glue:
- **A. Output** (`runtime/src/psp_audio_out.{h,cpp}` + `runtime/src/psp_audio_mixer.{h,cpp}`): a pure mixer (per-channel FIFOs of stereo s16, PSP volumes 0..0x8000) consumed by an SDL audio callback; the `sceAudio*Blocking` HLE pushes samples and blocks (yield + short sleep) while that channel holds more than ~2 grains.
- **B. SAS** (`runtime/src/hle/psp_sas_core.{h,cpp}`): port of PPSSPP `Core/HW/SasAudio.{h,cpp}` (VAG/PS-ADPCM decode, ADSR, pitch resampling, PCM voices, noise, mixing; reverb optional) driven by `runtime/src/hle/psp_hle_sas.cpp`, keeping the existing end-flag/grain behaviour the game already relies on.
- **C. ATRAC** (`runtime/third_party/at3_standalone/` vendored from PPSSPP `ext/at3_standalone`, + `runtime/src/hle/psp_atrac_ctx.{h,cpp}` + `runtime/src/hle/psp_hle_atrac.cpp`): port of PPSSPP `Core/HLE/AtracCtx2.cpp` semantics (the newer context) for the API the game imports, replacing the stubs in `psp_hle_utility.cpp`.

**Tech Stack:** C++17 (clang/llvm-mingw), SDL2 audio, CMake. Reference: PPSSPP master (GPL-2.0-or-later; ext/at3_standalone LGPL-2.1+) — compatible with this GPL-2.0 fork; keep their license headers on copied files.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-patapon3-native-port-design.md` §6.

## Review Focus
1. Blocking output must not deadlock the cooperative scheduler or starve other PSP threads (block = yield + sleep, never a raw condvar wait on the game thread).
2. SDL device absent/failed (no audio hardware) → the game keeps running at the right pace (fall back to timing-only blocking).
3. ATRAC streaming buffer wrap-around and loop points (SetLoopNum, ResetPlayPosition, GetBufferInfoForResetting) — Patapon chains music fragments on beat; off-by-one here desyncs the rhythm.
4. Guest pointers from the game (VAG addresses, output buffers) outside RAM → no host crash (use masked/NULL-guarded access).
5. Concurrency: sceAudio channels are fed from several PSP threads; mixer state must be thread-safe.
